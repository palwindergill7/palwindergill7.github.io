About

Analysis first, then the model.

I work at the intersection of business intelligence and applied machine learning. The Power BI side of my work is about giving people self-serve answers — dashboards where the next question is one click away. The statistics underneath make sure those answers hold up.

On the ML side I'm drawn to systems you can inspect: image search that shows its reasoning, transcription that runs on the edge instead of a black-box API. If a model can't explain itself, it hasn't finished its job.

The fastest way to reach me or see what I'm building is GitHub.
